ServiceM8 Add-on Yeastar SMS Gateway

SMS Dashboard

Automated & manual SMS for field-service businesses — inside ServiceM8

Product overview for partners & clients · Version 1.0 · Demo screenshots included

A cloud-hosted SMS platform that lives inside ServiceM8. Staff manage templates, automation rules, delivery logs, and customer replies from one dashboard. Messages go out through the customer’s own Yeastar TG400 gateway (their SIMs, their numbers) — no third-party SMS credit middleman.

What it does

When a job is created, a technician goes en route, or a job is completed, the system can automatically text the customer. Staff can also open any job and send a one-off SMS with a live preview. Customer replies appear in an inbox matched to the job by phone number.

Automatic job SMS

Rules fire on job created, status change, en route, or completed — using your message templates.

Manual Send SMS

From any job card: pick recipient + template, preview, send — without leaving ServiceM8.

Two-way inbox

Inbound replies from the Yeastar gateway show up in a conversation-style inbox.

Delivery log & analytics

See what was sent, status, and 7-day volume. Test the gateway from Settings.

Who it’s for

  • Trades and field-service businesses on ServiceM8 that own (or will buy) a Yeastar TG400
  • Teams that want branded SMS from their own SIM numbers, not a shared SaaS sender ID
  • Owners who don’t want a PC running 24/7 in the office — the app runs in the cloud

How it works (simple)

1. ServiceM8 job event or staff action → cloud SMS app
2. App loads full job + customer from ServiceM8 API
3. Rules/templates build the message → Yeastar TG400 sends via office SIMs
4. Customer replies → shown in Inbox

ServiceM8 → Cloud app (AWS) → Office router → Yeastar TG400
